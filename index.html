Gregory Levy

Hi there! I am a DPhil candidate in Economics at the University of Oxford (Magdalen College). I am also affiliated with FLAIR in Oxford, as well as the LSE Department of Management. My supervisors are Maximilian Kasy, Noam Yuchtman, and Jakob Foerster.

I develop statistical and machine learning methods for high-dimensional empirical research, and study the theoretical foundations of both ML and economic modelling. I apply these tools to questions in political economy, including political representation, belief formation, and institutional change.

I help organise the Machine Learning and Economics group at Oxford, and am a co-founder of the UK Graduate Econometrics Network (UK-GEN).
